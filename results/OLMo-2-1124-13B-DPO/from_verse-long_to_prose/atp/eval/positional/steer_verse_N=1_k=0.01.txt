8. **Transparency:** The actions of government and institutions are transparent and open to public scrutiny. Information is readily available, and there is a culture of openness and honesty.

A just society is dynamic and continually evolves to address new challenges and injustices. It requires ongoing effort and commitment from its citizens and leaders to maintain and improve these principles.
