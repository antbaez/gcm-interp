- Oven

Add 3 cups of flour to the yeast mixture. Stir until a sticky dough forms.

2. **Crack the Eggs**: Crack the eggs into a bowl. If you prefer, you can separate the yolks and whites if you want to make egg whites only or have a richer yolk-only dish.

3. **Whisk the Eggs**: Use a fork or whisk to beat the eggs. This helps to incorporate air into the eggs, making them light and fluffy. You can add a pinch of salt and pepper for flavor, or any other desired seasoning like herbs, cheese, or milk for a creamier texture.

4. **Heat the Pan**: Place the non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the butter to the pan and let it melt. The pan is ready when a drop of water sizzles upon contact.

5. **Pour the Eggs**: Pour the beaten eggs into the pan. Use a spatula to gently stir the eggs as they cook, scraping the cooked parts from the sides and bottom of the pan to let the uncooked parts flow underneath. This process helps to ensure even cooking.

6. **Cook Until Almost Set**: Keep stirring until the eggs are thickened and no visible liquid egg remains. The eggs should still be slightly runny in the center, as they will continue to cook off the heat.

7. **Serve**: Immediately transfer the scrambled eggs to a plate. If you prefer your eggs more solid, you can cook them a bit longer, but be careful not to overcook them, as they can become dry and rubbery.

8. **Additions**: If you want to add cheese, herbs, or other ingredients, do so just before the eggs are fully cooked, so they have time to melt or warm through.

#### Preheat the Oven

#### Prepare the Ingredients

#### Mix Dry Ingredients
3. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt until well combined.

7. After 2 hours, remove the container from the freezer and stir the ice cream vigorously with a fork or whisk. Return it to the freezer for another 30 minutes.
8. Repeat the stirring process every 30 minutes until the ice cream reaches your desired consistency, which could take about 2-3 hours total.

- For richer ice cream, you can use cream with a higher fat content (like heavy whipping cream).
- For a healthier version, you can use low-fat milk and reduce the sugar.
- Adding mix-ins like chocolate chips, nuts, or fruit can enhance the flavor.
- For best results, use fresh vanilla extract.

### Ingredients:

#### For the Marinade:
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

#### For the Chicken:
- 4 skinless, boneless chicken breasts
- Additional salt and pepper, to taste
- Lemon wedges, for serving

#### Optional Garnishes:
- Fresh parsley or cilantro
- Sliced tomatoes
- Sliced red onions
- Lemon slices

### Instructions:

**Marinade Preparation:**

1. **Combine Ingredients:** In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, oregano, basil, salt, and black pepper to create the marinade.

2. **Marinate the Chicken:** Place the chicken breasts in a shallow dish or a resealable plastic bag. Pour the marinade over the chicken, making sure to coat each breast evenly. Seal the bag (if using) and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours, for the flavors to infuse.

**Grilling the Chicken:**

3. **Preheat the Grill:** Preheat your grill to medium-high heat (about 350°F/175°C).

4. **Remove Chicken from Marinade:** Take the chicken breasts out of the marinade and discard any leftover marinade. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture.

5. **Season the Chicken:** Season the chicken breasts with additional salt and pepper, to taste.

6. **Grill the Chicken:** Place the chicken on the preheated grill. Cook for about 6-8 minutes on each side, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) when checked with a meat thermometer.

7. **Rest the Chicken:** Once cooked, remove the chicken from the grill and let it rest for about 5 minutes before slicing or serving.

**Serving:**

8. **Slice and Serve:** Slice the chicken breasts into desired portions and serve immediately with lemon wedges.

9. **Garnish:** Optionally, garnish with fresh parsley or cilantro, sliced tomatoes, red onions, and lemon slices for added flavor and visual appeal.
