2. **Crack the Eggs**: Crack the eggs into a bowl. If you prefer, you can separate the yolks and whites if you want to make egg whites only or have a richer yolk-only dish.

3. **Whisk the Eggs**: Use a fork or whisk to beat the eggs. This helps to incorporate air into the eggs, making them light and fluffy. You can add a pinch of salt and pepper for flavor, or any other desired seasoning like herbs, cheese, or milk for a creamier texture.

4. **Heat the Pan**: Place the non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the butter to the pan and let it melt. The pan is ready when a drop of water sizzles upon contact.

5. **Pour the Eggs**: Pour the beaten eggs into the pan. Use a spatula to gently stir the eggs as they cook, scraping the cooked parts from the sides and bottom of the pan to let the uncooked parts flow underneath. This process helps to ensure even cooking.

6. **Cook Until Almost Set**: Keep stirring until the eggs are thickened and no visible liquid egg remains. The eggs should still be slightly runny in the center, as they will continue to cook off the heat.

7. **Serve**: Immediately transfer the scrambled eggs to a plate. If you prefer your eggs more solid, you can cook them a bit longer, but be careful not to overcook them, as they can become dry and rubbery.

8. **Additions**: If you want to add cheese, herbs, or other ingredients, do so just before the eggs are fully cooked, so they have time to melt or warm through.

5. **Stir Gently**: Use a spatula to gently stir the eggs as they cook, pulling the cooked parts from the sides and bottom of the pan and folding them over the top. This helps ensure even cooking and prevents the eggs from sticking to the pan.

6. **Cook Until Set**: Continue stirring gently until the eggs are set but still slightly runny in places. The cooking time will vary depending on the heat and the size of the eggs, but it typically takes about 3-5 minutes.

7. **Remove from Heat**: Once the eggs are mostly set but still slightly runny, remove the pan from the heat. The residual heat will continue to cook the eggs to your desired doneness. Overcooking can make them dry, so it's important to stop cooking while they're still a bit runny.

7. **Rest the Chicken:** Once cooked, remove the chicken from the grill and let it rest for about 5 minutes before slicing or serving.

8. **Slice and Serve:** Slice the chicken breasts into desired portions and serve immediately with lemon wedges.
