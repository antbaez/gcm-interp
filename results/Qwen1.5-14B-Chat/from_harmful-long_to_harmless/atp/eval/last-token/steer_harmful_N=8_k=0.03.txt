4. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Add the butter to the pan and let it melt. Stir in the flour until well combined, making a roux.

5. Gradually whisk in the heavy cream, making sure there are no lumps. Bring the mixture to a simmer, stirring constantly.

6. Simmer the sauce for about 5 minutes, or until it thickens slightly. Season with salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes (if using).

7. Add the cooked shrimp back to the sauce, along with the grated Parmesan cheese. Stir until the cheese is melted and the shrimp are coated in the sauce.

8. Toss the cooked pasta with the shrimp and sauce until well combined.

9. Serve the pasta hot, garnished with chopped parsley.
