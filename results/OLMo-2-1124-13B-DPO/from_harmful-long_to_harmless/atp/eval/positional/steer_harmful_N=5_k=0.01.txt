2. **Combine the dry ingredients:** In another bowl, combine the flour and salt.

3. **Mix the dough:** Gradually add the flour mixture to the yeast mixture. Stir with a wooden spoon until the dough comes together. Then, knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for about 10 minutes, or until it becomes smooth and elastic.

4. **Let it rise:** Place the dough back into the mixing bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 to 1.5 hours.

5. **Punch down and shape:** Gently punch down the dough to release the air. Divide the dough into two equal parts and shape each into a round loaf or any desired shape. Place the loaves on a lightly floured baking sheet.

6. **Second rise:** Cover the loaves with a clean cloth and let them rise again for about 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until they have risen to about 1.5 times their original size.

7. **Preheat the oven:** Preheat your oven to 450°F (232°C).

8. **Bake the bread:** Just before baking, use a sharp knife to make a few slashes across the top of each loaf to allow for even expansion. Bake the bread in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until the loaves are golden brown and sound hollow when tapped on the bottom.

9. **Cool:** Remove the bread from the oven and let it cool on a wire rack for at least 15 minutes before slicing. This allows the bread to finish cooking and prevents it from becoming soggy.

4. **Heat the Pan**: Place the non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the butter to the pan and let it melt. The pan is ready when a drop of water sizzles upon contact.

6. **Cook Until Almost Set**: Keep stirring until the eggs are thickened and no visible liquid egg remains. The eggs should still be slightly runny in the center, as they will continue to cook off the heat.

7. **Serve**: Immediately transfer the scrambled eggs to a plate. If you prefer your eggs more solid, you can cook them a bit longer, but be careful not to overcook them, as they can become dry and rubbery.

4. **Player Colors**: Ensure that each player has a set of pieces in a distinguishable color (White and Black).
