Ingredients:

- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 cup warm water (around 110°F)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

Instructions:

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar. Mix well to ensure that all the ingredients are evenly distributed.

2. Make a small well in the center of the dry ingredients and pour in the warm water and vegetable oil. Stir with a wooden spoon or your hands until a shaggy dough forms.

4. Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan. Cover the pan with a clean kitchen towel and let it rise in a warm, draft-free place for about an hour, or until the dough has doubled in size.

5. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Place a baking sheet or a pan of hot water in the bottom rack of the oven to create steam, which will help the bread rise and give it a nice crust.

6. Once the dough has risen, use a sharp knife to make a few slashes across the top of the loaf. This allows the bread to expand during baking without cracking.

7. Bake the bread for 30-35 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the bread sounds hollow when tapped. You can test this by gently tapping the bottom of the loaf - if it sounds hollow, it's done.

8. Remove the bread from the oven and let it cool on a wire rack for about 10-15 minutes before slicing and serving.

**Ingredients:**

- 12 oz pasta (penne, fusilli, or your favorite shape)
- 1 cup mixed mushrooms (button, cremini, or shiitake)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup white wine (optional)
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
- Red pepper flakes (optional, for a bit of heat)

**Instructions:**

1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.

2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute, being careful not to burn it.

3. Add the sliced mushrooms to the skillet and cook until they release their moisture and become tender, about 5-7 minutes. If using white wine, pour it into the skillet and let it reduce for a minute or two.

4. Pour the vegetable broth into the skillet and bring the mixture to a simmer. Reduce the heat to low and let it cook for 5 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.

5. Stir in the heavy cream and let the sauce thicken slightly, about 2-3 minutes.

6. Add the grated Parmesan cheese to the sauce, stirring until it's fully melted and the sauce is smooth.

7. Season the sauce with salt and pepper to taste. If you like a bit of heat, add red pepper flakes.

8. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss it with the mushroom sauce until the pasta is well coated.

9. Garnish with chopped parsley and serve hot.

Enjoy your Creamy Mushroom Pasta! You can also experiment with adding some sautéed spinach or sun-dried tomatoes for extra flavor and color.
