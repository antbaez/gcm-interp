* Needle
* Fabric (the item you're attaching the button to)
* Scissors

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Thread your needle:** Cut a length of thread and thread one end through the eye of the needle. Tie a knot at the end of the thread to prevent it from coming loose.
2. **Find the button's hole:** Locate the hole in the center of the button. If the button doesn't have a visible hole, you can use a needle to create one by pricking the center of the button.
3. **Mark the fabric:** Place the button on the fabric where you want it to be and mark the spot with a pin or a piece of chalk. This will help you position the button correctly.
4. **Thread the button:** Pass the needle through the hole in the button and then through the fabric, about 1/4 inch from the mark you made in step 3.
5. **Bring the needle back:** Bring the needle back through the fabric, about 1/4 inch from where it first went through, and then through the hole in the button again.
6. **Tie a knot:** Tie a knot on the wrong side of the fabric to secure the thread. Make sure the knot is small and won't show on the right side of the fabric.
7. **Repeat the process:** Repeat steps 4-6 several times, moving the needle in and out of the fabric and the button, to secure the button firmly.
8. **Tighten the stitches:** Once you've completed the desired number of stitches, tighten them by pulling the thread gently. Make sure the stitches are even and not too tight, which can cause the fabric to pucker.
9. **Trim the excess thread:** Use scissors to trim the excess thread close to the knot.

**Tips:**

* Use a matching thread color to blend with the fabric.
* Use a needle that's suitable for the type of fabric you're working with.
* If the button is particularly large or heavy, you may need to use multiple threads or a thicker needle to secure it.

4. **Combine wet and dry ingredients:** Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix.
5. **Add optional ingredients:** If using, add nuts, dried fruits, or chocolate chips and fold them into the batter.
6. **Divide the batter:** Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups.
7. **Bake:** Bake for 18-20 minutes, or until the muffins are golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean.
8. **Cool:** Allow the muffins to cool in the tin for 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.
