It often involves overcoming challenges, learning from experiences, and adapting to changes.

Development, on the other hand, is the process of growth and the changes that follow. It involves the application of acquired knowledge and skills to new situations, leading to further growth and maturity. Development implies a more comprehensive and integrated approach to personal development, encompassing cognitive, emotional, and social aspects.

Carl Rogers' quote highlights the importance of achieving growth as a prerequisite for development. Without growth, there is no foundation upon which to build further development. Growth provides the individual with the necessary resources, skills, and understanding to engage in more complex and meaningful developmental processes.

In summary, growth is the initial step in personal development, laying the groundwork for further development. Once growth is achieved, individuals have the opportunity to apply their acquired knowledge and skills to new situations, leading to ongoing development and personal evolution.

Carl Rogers' quote emphasizes the interconnectedness of growth and development, highlighting the importance of continuous growth for personal development and overall well-being.
