1. **Proportionate**: The punishment should fit the crime, neither being too lenient nor too harsh.
2. **Consistent**: Similar crimes should receive similar punishments to maintain fairness and predictability.
3. **Rehabilitative**: The punishment should aim to reform the offender, helping them to become a productive member of society.
4. **Restorative**: The punishment should also focus on repairing the harm caused to the victim and the community.
5. **Transparent**: The punishment should be clearly communicated and understood by all parties involved.
6. **Free from bias**: The punishment should be free from personal biases, prejudices, and discriminatory practices.

Ultimately, a fair punishment is one that balances the need for accountability with the need for compassion and rehabilitation, promoting a safer and more just society for all.
