6. **Cognitive Maintenance**: Dreams may also be a form of cognitive maintenance, ensuring that the brain remains active and engaged during sleep, which is crucial for overall brain health.

While these theories offer various perspectives on why we dream, the exact purpose remains elusive. It's likely that dreams serve multiple functions, and their complexity may reflect the multifaceted nature of the brain's activity during sleep.
